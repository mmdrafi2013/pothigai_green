SooqFlow Marketplace Terms of Use

Version: 1.0 production-candidate · Effective date: 26 September 2026

Important: SooqFlow is designed as a B2B marketplace facilitator. Unless a transaction screen expressly states otherwise, SooqFlow is not the seller, purchaser, manufacturer, owner of goods, freight carrier, insurer, lender, bank, or payment service provider. The registered businesses entering a transaction remain responsible for their own contractual obligations.

1. Eligibility and business verification

Users must be legally capable of acting for the business they register. Suppliers and transporters must maintain valid commercial, sectoral, vehicle, insurance, operating-card and other licences required for the activity they perform. SooqFlow may suspend access where verification expires or cannot be confirmed.

2. Buyer responsibilities

The buyer is responsible for describing the requirement accurately, reviewing quotations, confirming technical suitability, approving deviations, selecting the supplier, honouring accepted commercial terms, and making payment according to the agreed terms. For logistics, the buyer proposes the intended transport method or vehicle unless the order expressly assigns transport arrangement to the supplier.

3. Supplier responsibilities

The supplier is responsible for truthful stock statements, specifications, quantity, price, taxes, quotation validity, delivery readiness, packaging, certificates and documents. A supplier must not label goods as available when it knows or reasonably should know that the goods are unavailable. Supplier acceptance of a buyer-proposed vehicle confirms only the suitability of that proposed transport for the goods and loading method; it does not transfer the carrier's driving or carriage responsibilities to the supplier.

4. Stock status and verification labels

"Supplier declared" means the quantity was stated by the supplier and not independently verified. "Evidence-backed" means recent supporting material was provided. "System-synchronised" means inventory information came from an integrated source. "Physically verified" applies only where a physical verification step was completed. No label guarantees future availability after reservation, sale, damage, system error or other change.

5. Quotations and orders

Quotations must state material description, quantity, unit/total price, applicable tax, validity and delivery/collection terms. Submitted quotation changes remain auditable. A marketplace RFQ is not itself a purchase order. An order becomes binding only according to the buyer and supplier's accepted commercial terms and applicable law.

6. Transport workflow

Where the buyer proposes logistics, the supplier must respond Suitable, Suitable with Conditions, or Not Suitable. Marketplace transport RFQs are released only after the required suitability confirmation. The buyer may choose self-transport or an eligible marketplace transporter. Transporters remain responsible for vehicle legality, driver competence, safe carriage, load limits, route/permit compliance and delivery performance. Special or exceptional cargo may require additional approvals.

7. Pickup, delivery and evidence

Pickup timestamps, condition photos, OTP/QR acknowledgements, driver status and proof of delivery may be used as transaction evidence. Users must not falsify, alter or suppress such evidence. Photos and signatures should be limited to what is reasonably required for the transaction.

8. Cancellations, delays and disputes

The app may record cancellation reason, no-show, stock failure, delay, damage claim and dispute outcome as performance information. The platform may facilitate communication and evidence exchange, but unless explicitly agreed in a separate service contract it does not guarantee settlement of a commercial dispute. Parties retain their legal rights under applicable Oman law.

9. Ratings and trust metrics

Trust indicators may use verified transaction records, stock accuracy, fulfilment, cancellation, response, delivery and dispute data. They are decision-support indicators, not guarantees of future performance. Manipulating ratings, creating sham transactions or using fake accounts is prohibited.

10. Payments

Unless a licensed payment partner is expressly identified in the transaction, payments are made directly between buyer and supplier/transporter. SooqFlow must not be treated as holding escrow, deposits or wallet balances merely because a transaction was initiated through the app.

11. Prohibited conduct

Users must not list illegal, counterfeit, stolen or prohibited goods; misrepresent licences or stock; manipulate quotations; impersonate another business; upload malicious content; misuse personal data; interfere with the platform; or use the service for fraud, money laundering or unlawful transport.

12. Platform availability and limitation

SooqFlow may suspend features for security, regulatory, maintenance or verification reasons. To the extent permitted by law, the platform does not warrant uninterrupted service, that a supplier will perform, or that a transporter will complete a job. Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded.

13. Intellectual property and uploaded content

Users retain ownership of their lawful business content but grant the platform the limited rights required to host, process, match, transmit and display it for the service. Users confirm they are authorised to upload technical documents, images and certificates.

14. Privacy

Personal data is handled under the SooqFlow Privacy Policy. Marketing messages require a separate choice and are not a condition of using core marketplace functions.

15. Suspension and termination

Accounts may be restricted or suspended for fraud, repeated false-stock incidents, expired transport documentation, regulatory concerns, abusive conduct, security risk or material breach. Where appropriate, the user may be given an opportunity to respond.

16. Governing framework

These terms are intended for operation in the Sultanate of Oman and should be interpreted with applicable Oman laws and regulations. Mandatory statutory rights prevail over any conflicting contractual provision.

17. Operator details before public launch

The final Play Store release must display the licensed operator's exact legal name, Commercial Registration number, business address, support email and telephone. Those details must be inserted after the operating entity and licences are issued; they must not be invented in the app.
