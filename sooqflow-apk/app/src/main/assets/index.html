Core workflow

1. Buyer raises requirement
Text, photo, PDF/Excel or quick-buy.
2. Supplier confirms stock and quote
Availability is never treated as verified without evidence.
3. Buyer proposes logistics
Vehicle/type/time proposed by buyer.
4. Supplier confirms suitability
Suitable / suitable with conditions / not suitable.
5. Buyer chooses transport path
Self transport or hire enrolled marketplace transporter.
6. Pickup → transit → POD
Timestamped evidence reduces disputes.
Small suppliers remain free on the basic plan and can quote manually without ERP or catalogue uploads.

Compliance & administration

Launch guardrails

  • Verify supplier/company CR and business identity.
  • Verify transporter licence, Vehicle Operating Card, insurance and expiry dates.
  • Keep marketplace, supplier, buyer and transporter responsibilities explicit.
  • Use consent, privacy, retention and access controls for personal/business data.
  • Do not hold customer money directly without the appropriate licensed payment structure.
  • Keep evidence for stock confirmation, quotation, pickup and POD.

Legal & privacy

Marketplace role

SooqFlow is designed as a B2B marketplace facilitator. Supplier, buyer and transporter obligations remain separately recorded for each transaction.

Production launch status

Final public release must insert the licensed operator's legal name, CR number, address and support/privacy contacts, and must obtain the required Oman business/e-commerce/transport-app approvals for the chosen operating model.

Before using SooqFlow

This B2B marketplace records supplier, buyer and transport responsibilities and may process business contact and transaction data.
