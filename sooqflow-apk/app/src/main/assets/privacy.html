SooqFlow Privacy Policy

Version: 1.0 production-candidate · Effective date: 26 September 2026

This policy is designed around Oman's Personal Data Protection framework. The production operator must complete the controller identity, processing register, data-retention schedule, processor contracts, breach process and any required permits/assessments before public processing begins.

1. Data we may process

Business-account details; authorised contact name, email and telephone; login/security data; company verification records; supplier and transporter licences; vehicle/driver information where required for transport; RFQs; quotations; orders; stock confirmations; pickup/delivery evidence; approximate or precise location only where required and permitted; device/security logs; support and dispute records.

2. Purposes

Account creation and verification; matching buyers with suppliers and transporters; stock and quotation workflows; transport suitability and booking; pickup/POD evidence; fraud prevention; regulatory compliance; customer support; dispute handling; security; service analytics; and marketing only where the user has separately opted in.

3. Legal basis and consent

Where Oman law requires consent, SooqFlow will request it in a clear form and record the choice. Consent for promotional messages is separate from acceptance of the Terms and Privacy Policy. Where processing is permitted on another lawful basis or exemption, the platform will document that basis.

4. Location

Location is not collected merely because the app is open. When a logistics feature needs location, the app should explain the purpose and request the relevant device permission. Users may deny location and enter addresses manually where feasible.

5. Sharing

Transaction information may be shared with the buyer, supplier and transporter to the extent needed to perform the selected workflow. Data may also be processed by contracted hosting, communications, security, analytics or verification providers under appropriate contractual controls. The platform does not sell personal data.

6. Cross-border processing

Before transferring personal data outside Oman where an assessment is required, the controller should assess the protection offered by the recipient country/provider and document safeguards.

7. Retention

Production retention periods must be documented by category. Data should be kept only for as long as required for the transaction, statutory records, legitimate dispute/audit needs, security, or other lawful purposes, then securely deleted or anonymised.

8. Data-subject rights

Users should be provided a channel to exercise rights available under applicable Oman law, including access/correction and other applicable rights. Where an account exists, the production service should provide an account-deletion/request mechanism and explain records that must legally be retained.

9. Marketing communications

No advertising or promotional messages should be sent unless the required consent has been obtained. A user who withdraws marketing consent should be removed from future marketing communications promptly.

10. Security

Production controls should include encryption in transit, least-privilege access, multi-factor authentication for sensitive roles, audit logs, secure credential storage, verification for bank-detail changes, backups, vulnerability management and incident response.

11. Breach response

The production operator must assess security incidents and notify the competent authority and affected data subjects when required by applicable law and regulation.

12. Children

SooqFlow is a business marketplace and is not intended for children. Business users must be authorised adults acting for their organisation.

13. Automated processing

Matching, trust scores and recommendations may use automated processing. These outputs are decision-support tools. Material decisions such as suspension or high-impact dispute action should include appropriate review and an avenue to challenge errors.

14. Contact/controller details before public launch

The final policy must state the licensed controller/operator's legal name, CR number, address, privacy/support email and telephone. These details must be inserted after company registration and must not be fabricated.
